FAQ
Q: How do I use the wellness tools on this site?
A: Explore the different tools like mindfulness timers, mini exercises, and activity trackers. Each tool has simple instructions to help you build healthy habits step by step.
Q: Are the quizzes on this site meant for fun or self-assessment?
A: Both! The quizzes help you reflect on your mental and physical health, learn new wellness tips, and discover areas where you can improve.
Q: Can these tips help reduce stress from schoolwork?
A: Yes! Practicing mindfulness, taking mini-breaks, staying active, and following healthy routines can all reduce stress and improve focus.
Q: What should I do if I need more help than the site provides?
A: If you’re struggling with persistent stress, anxiety, or health issues, contact a school counselor, healthcare provider, or mental health professional.
