MENTAL HEALTH

WHY IT MATTERS

Mental health affects how we think, feel, and act every day. It influences how we handle stress, make choices, and build relationships. Taking care of your mind is just as important as caring for your body—it helps you stay balanced, productive, and resilient in life.

Stress

Stress is a natural reaction to challenges, but too much of it can affect sleep, focus, and mood. Recognizing your stress triggers early helps prevent burnout. Practicing breathing exercises, journaling, or even short walks can reduce tension and reset your mind.

Focus & Motivation

Good mental health keeps your mind clear and your goals in sight. When you’re mentally well, it’s easier to stay organized, focused, and inspired. Break big tasks into small steps, celebrate progress, and keep a healthy routine to maintain motivation.

Relaxation

Relaxation helps your brain recover from constant activity. Taking breaks to meditate, stretch, or listen to calming music lowers stress hormones and improves overall well-being. Even 5 minutes of mindfulness can make a difference.

Tips

Maintain a regular sleep schedule Stay active — even light exercise boosts mood Eat balanced meals and stay hydrated Talk about your feelings with someone you trust Take short breaks when overwhelmed Limit screen time before bed

Quick Activities

Deep Breathing: Inhale 4 sec, hold 4 sec, exhale 4 sec Gratitude Note: Write down 3 things you’re thankful for Mini Stretch: Roll shoulders, neck, and wrists for 1 min Mindful Moment: Focus on sounds, sights, or sensations around you Hydration Break: Drink water mindfully—pause and reset
